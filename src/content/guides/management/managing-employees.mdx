## Employee Lifecycle

The employee lifecycle in the JDC Portal follows these stages:

1. **Creation** — A new employee record is created
2. **Onboarding** — The employee completes their account setup
3. **Active** — The employee is fully operational
4. **Updates** — Ongoing profile and employment changes
5. **Offboarding** — Employee departure and record archival

## Creating a New Employee

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Navigate to Employees">
    Go to **Employees** in the **People** sidebar section, then click the **New Employee** button.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Personal Information">
    Enter the employee's **first name**, **last name**, **email address**, and **phone number**. These are required fields.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Address Details">
    Fill in the employee's address information including street, city, state, and zip code.
  </Step>
  <Step number={4} title="Employment Details">
    Set the employee's **department**, **position**, **hire date**, and any other relevant employment information.
  </Step>
  <Step number={5} title="Review & Save">
    Double-check all entered information, then click **Save** to create the employee record.
  </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Required Fields">
  Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required. The form will highlight any missing or invalid fields when you try to save.
</Callout>

## Editing an Employee

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Find the Employee">
    From the Employees list, search for and click on the employee you want to edit.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Enter Edit Mode">
    On the employee detail page, click the **Edit** button to enable editing of the fields.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Make Changes">
    Update the relevant fields. Changes to employment details (department, position) are tracked for audit purposes.
  </Step>
  <Step number={4} title="Save Changes">
    Click **Save** to apply your changes. The system will validate all fields before saving.
  </Step>
</StepList>

## Employee Status Management

Employees can have different statuses throughout their time with the organization:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Awaiting account setup or approval |
| **Onboarding** | In the process of completing account setup |
| **Active** | Fully operational employee |
| **Inactive** | Temporarily deactivated |
| **Terminated** | Employment ended |
| **Archived** | Record preserved for historical purposes |

<Callout variant="warning" title="Status Changes">
  Changing an employee's status may affect their portal access. For example, setting a status to **Terminated** or **Inactive** will prevent them from logging in.
</Callout>

## Best Practices

- **Keep records current** — Update employee information promptly when changes occur
- **Use accurate departments** — Correct department assignments help with reporting and access control
- **Document important changes** — Use internal notes when making significant status or role changes
- **Coordinate with Admin** — For user account changes (login access, roles), work with your system administrator
