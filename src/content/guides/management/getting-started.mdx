## Welcome, Manager

As a **Management** user, you have access to manage employees and oversee key company operations. This guide introduces your dashboard and primary workflows.

## Your Dashboard

Your dashboard provides a management-focused view with:

- **Employee Overview** — Key statistics about the team
- **Recent Activity** — Latest employee changes and onboarding events
- **Quick Actions** — Fast access to common management tasks

## Key Management Tasks

### Employee Directory

The **Employees** page is your primary tool for managing your team.

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="View All Employees">
    Navigate to **Employees** in the **People** sidebar section. You'll see a list of all employee records with key details like name, department, and status.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Search & Filter">
    Use the search bar to find employees by name. Filter by department or status to narrow your view.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="View Employee Details">
    Click on an employee to view their complete profile including personal information, employment details, and activity history.
  </Step>
</StepList>

### Creating New Employees

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Start the Process">
    From the Employees page, click **New Employee** or the create button.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Enter Employee Information">
    Fill in the employee's personal details, contact information, and employment information such as department and position.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Save & Onboard">
    After saving, the system will create the employee record. If the employee needs portal access, coordinate with an Admin to set up their user account.
  </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout variant="info" title="Employee vs. User">
  An **Employee** record stores employment data (department, position, etc.). A **User** account provides portal login access. They are linked but managed separately — employees are created by Managers, while user accounts are created by Admins.
</Callout>

## Vendor Management

Managers may also have access to view and manage vendor information:

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="View Vendors">
    Navigate to **Vendors** in the **People** sidebar section to see all registered vendors.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Review Vendor Profiles">
    Click on a vendor to view their company information, certifications, and status.
  </Step>
</StepList>
