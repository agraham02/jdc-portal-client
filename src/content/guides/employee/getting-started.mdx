## Welcome to the JDC Portal

As a **JDC Employee**, the portal gives you access to important company resources, your personal profile, and communication tools. This quick start guide will help you get up and running.

## First Steps After Login

<StepList>
    <Step number={1} title="Complete Your Profile">
        Navigate to **Profile** in the sidebar footer. Review and update your
        personal information including your name, phone number, and address. A
        complete profile helps your team stay connected.
    </Step>
    <Step number={2} title="Set Up Notifications">
        Go to **Settings** → **Notifications** to configure how you receive
        updates. You can choose between in-app notifications and email alerts.
    </Step>
    <Step number={3} title="Explore the Dashboard">
        Your **Dashboard** is your home page. It shows relevant information and
        quick actions based on your role. Take a moment to familiarize yourself
        with the layout.
    </Step>
</StepList>

## Accessing HR Resources

The HR Resources section contains important company documents like policies, handbooks, and forms.

<StepList>
    <Step number={1} title="Navigate to HR Resources">
        Click **HR Resources** in the **HR** sidebar section.
    </Step>
    <Step number={2} title="Browse Documents">
        On the **Documents** tab, you'll find all available HR documents
        organized by category. Use the search bar to find specific documents.
    </Step>
    <Step number={3} title="View Useful Links">
        Switch to the **Links** tab to find links to external resources like
        payroll systems, benefits portals, and training materials.
    </Step>
    <Step number={4} title="Download Documents">
        Click on any document to download it to your computer.
    </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Bookmark Important Docs">
    If there are documents you access frequently (like the employee handbook or
    leave request forms), bookmark the HR Resources page for quick access.
</Callout>

## Managing Your Account

### Updating Your Profile

<StepList>
    <Step number={1} title="Go to Profile">
        Click **Profile** in the sidebar footer.
    </Step>
    <Step number={2} title="Edit Personal Info">
        You can update your name, phone number, email, and address. Click
        **Save** after making changes.
    </Step>
</StepList>

### Changing Your Password

<StepList>
    <Step number={1} title="Go to Profile">
        Navigate to your **Profile** page.
    </Step>
    <Step number={2} title="Find the Password Section">
        Scroll to the **Password** section on your profile page.
    </Step>
    <Step number={3} title="Update Password">
        Enter your current password and your new password. Click **Save** to
        update.
    </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout variant="warning" title="Security Tip">
    Choose a strong password that you don't use on other websites. Never share
    your password with anyone.
</Callout>

## Requesting Account Deletion

You can ask to have your account deleted from **Settings**. For most employees this opens a request that a manager or administrator reviews first — you do not lose access immediately, and you can cancel the request at any time before it's approved.

<StepList>
    <Step number={1} title="Open Settings">
        Click **Settings** in the sidebar footer.
    </Step>
    <Step number={2} title="Open Danger Zone">
        Scroll to the **Danger Zone** section and click **Request Account
        Deletion**.
    </Step>
    <Step number={3} title="Confirm the Request">
        Read the warning and confirm. A notice will appear at the top of
        Settings showing the request is pending review.
    </Step>
    <Step number={4} title="Cancel If You Change Your Mind">
        While the request is pending — and even after it has been approved, up
        until the scheduled deletion date — you can click **Cancel Deletion
        Request** to restore your account.
    </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout variant="tip" title="What happens after approval">
    Once approved, your account is moved to an archived state and scheduled to
    be permanently anonymized after a grace period (typically 30 days for
    employees, 7 days for vendors). During the grace period you can still
    cancel. After anonymization, personal data is scrubbed and the account
    cannot be restored.
</Callout>
