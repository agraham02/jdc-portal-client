## Welcome to the JDC Portal

As a **JDC Employee**, the portal gives you access to important company resources, your personal profile, and communication tools. This quick start guide will help you get up and running.

## First Steps After Login

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Complete Your Profile">
    Navigate to **Profile** in the sidebar footer. Review and update your personal information including your name, phone number, and address. A complete profile helps your team stay connected.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Set Up Notifications">
    Go to **Settings** → **Notifications** to configure how you receive updates. You can choose between in-app notifications and email alerts.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Explore the Dashboard">
    Your **Dashboard** is your home page. It shows relevant information and quick actions based on your role. Take a moment to familiarize yourself with the layout.
  </Step>
</StepList>

## Accessing HR Resources

The HR Resources section contains important company documents like policies, handbooks, and forms.

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Navigate to HR Resources">
    Click **HR Resources** in the **HR** sidebar section.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Browse Documents">
    On the **Documents** tab, you'll find all available HR documents organized by category. Use the search bar to find specific documents.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="View Useful Links">
    Switch to the **Links** tab to find links to external resources like payroll systems, benefits portals, and training materials.
  </Step>
  <Step number={4} title="Download Documents">
    Click on any document to download it to your computer.
  </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Bookmark Important Docs">
  If there are documents you access frequently (like the employee handbook or leave request forms), bookmark the HR Resources page for quick access.
</Callout>

## Managing Your Account

### Updating Your Profile

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Go to Profile">
    Click **Profile** in the sidebar footer.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Edit Personal Info">
    You can update your name, phone number, email, and address. Click **Save** after making changes.
  </Step>
</StepList>

### Changing Your Password

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Go to Profile">
    Navigate to your **Profile** page.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Find the Password Section">
    Scroll to the **Password** section on your profile page.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Update Password">
    Enter your current password and your new password. Click **Save** to update.
  </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout variant="warning" title="Security Tip">
  Choose a strong password that you don't use on other websites. Never share your password with anyone.
</Callout>
