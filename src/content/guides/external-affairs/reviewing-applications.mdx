## Application Review Workflow

When a contract is published (Open status), vendors can submit applications. Your job is to review them, evaluate proposals, and ultimately award the contract to the best vendor.

The application flow:

| Step                    | Action                                                        |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Applications arrive** | Vendors submit proposals with required documents              |
| **Review**              | Evaluate each application's proposal and documents            |
| **Accept / Reject**     | Move promising applications to review, reject unsuitable ones |
| **Award**               | Select the winning vendor and award the contract              |

## Viewing Applications

<StepList>
    <Step number={1} title="Open the Contract">
        Navigate to **Contracts** and click on the contract you want to review.
        The contract detail page shows all submissions.
    </Step>
    <Step number={2} title="Find the Applications Section">
        Scroll down to the **Applications** section. You'll see a table showing
        all vendor submissions with their status, submission date, and proposal
        summary.
    </Step>
    <Step number={3} title="Filter by Status">
        Use the status filter dropdown to narrow down applications — view only
        **Submitted**, **Reviewed**, **Accepted**, or **Rejected** applications.
    </Step>
</StepList>

## Reviewing an Application

<StepList>
    <Step number={1} title="Open Application Details">
        Click the **eye icon** on any application row to view the full
        application details, including the vendor's proposal, submitted
        documents, and contact information.
    </Step>
    <Step number={2} title="Review the Proposal">
        Read the vendor's proposal details carefully. Consider how well it
        addresses your contract requirements, budget, and timeline.
    </Step>
    <Step number={3} title="Check Submitted Documents">
        Review all documents the vendor submitted. Verify they've provided all
        required documents as specified in the contract.
    </Step>
    <Step number={4} title="Add Internal Notes">
        Use the **Internal Notes** section to record your evaluation notes.
        These are only visible to staff — vendors cannot see them.
    </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Internal Notes">
    Internal notes are a great way to collaborate with colleagues during the
    review process. Add your assessment, concerns, or recommendations for each
    application.
</Callout>

## Accepting or Rejecting Applications

<StepList>
    <Step number={1} title="Open the Actions Menu">
        In the Applications table, click the **three-dot menu** (⋮) on the right
        side of any application row.
    </Step>
    <Step number={2} title="Accept or Reject">
        Choose **Accept** to move a promising application into the review pool,
        or **Reject** to decline it. Accepted applications are flagged for final
        selection.
    </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout variant="info" title="Status Changes">
    - **Accept** changes the status to "In Review" — the vendor sees their
    application is being considered. - **Reject** notifies the vendor their
    application was not selected. This is final.
</Callout>

## Awarding a Contract

<StepList>
    <Step number={1} title="Close the Contract">
        Before awarding, **close** the contract to stop accepting new
        applications. On the contract details page, click the **Close** button.
    </Step>
    <Step number={2} title="Select the Winner">
        From the Applications table, click the **three-dot menu** on the winning
        application and select **Award**.
    </Step>
    <Step number={3} title="Confirm the Award">
        Confirm your selection. The contract status changes to **Awarded**, and
        the winning vendor is notified.
    </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout variant="warning" title="Awarding Is Final">
    Once a contract is awarded, it cannot be changed. Make sure you've reviewed
    all applications thoroughly before making your decision.
</Callout>

## Best Practices

- **Review promptly** — Vendors appreciate timely feedback on their applications.
- **Use internal notes** — Document your evaluation criteria and reasoning for future reference.
- **Check all required documents** — Ensure vendors have submitted everything before accepting.
- **Close before awarding** — Always close applications before selecting a winner to prevent late submissions.
