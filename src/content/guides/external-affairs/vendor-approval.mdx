## Vendor Registration Flow

Vendors register through the public-facing registration page on the JDC Portal. Here's how the flow works:

1. **Vendor self-registers** — They fill out the registration form with company details
2. **Pending review** — The registration appears in your vendor list as **Pending**
3. **You review** — Evaluate the vendor's information and documents
4. **Approve or reject** — Make a decision based on your evaluation

## Reviewing Pending Vendors

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Navigate to Vendors">
    Go to **Vendors** in the **People** sidebar section. Look for vendors with a **Pending** status badge.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Open the Vendor Profile">
    Click on the pending vendor to view their complete registration information, including:
    - Company name and contact details
    - Business type and registration number
    - Company address
    - Any uploaded certifications or documents
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Verify Information">
    Review all submitted information carefully. Check that:
    - Company details are complete and reasonable
    - Contact information is valid
    - Any required certifications or documents are present
  </Step>
</StepList>

## Approving a Vendor

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Open the Vendor Profile">
    Navigate to the vendor's detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Click Approve">
    Click the **Approve** button. The vendor's status will change to **Active**.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Vendor Gets Notified">
    The vendor receives a notification that their registration has been approved. They can now browse and apply for open contracts.
  </Step>
</StepList>

## Rejecting a Vendor

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Open the Vendor Profile">
    Navigate to the vendor's detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Click Reject">
    Click the **Reject** button. You may be asked to provide a reason for rejection.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Vendor Gets Notified">
    The vendor receives a notification that their registration was not approved.
  </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Best Practice">
  Review vendor registrations promptly. Quick turnaround times encourage vendor participation and keep the procurement pipeline healthy.
</Callout>

<Callout variant="info" title="Re-registration">
  If a vendor's registration is rejected, they may be able to re-register with corrected information, depending on the portal configuration.
</Callout>
