## Welcome to Procurement

As an **External Affairs** team member, you manage the procurement workflow — from publishing contracts to reviewing vendor applications and awarding work. This guide introduces the key concepts.

## How Procurement Works

The procurement workflow follows these steps:

1. **Create a Contract** — Define the scope, requirements, and terms
2. **Open for Applications** — Publish the contract for vendors to apply
3. **Review Applications** — Evaluate vendor submissions
4. **Award the Contract** — Select the winning vendor
5. **Close the Contract** — Mark the contract as complete

## Key Pages

### Contracts

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="View All Contracts">
    Navigate to **Contracts** in the **Procurement** sidebar section. You'll see all contracts with their status, title, and key dates.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Filter by Status">
    Use the status filter to view contracts in specific stages: **Draft**, **Open**, **Closed**, or **Awarded**.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="View Contract Details">
    Click on any contract to see its full details, including description, requirements, attached documents, and vendor applications.
  </Step>
</StepList>

### Vendors

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="View All Vendors">
    Navigate to **Vendors** in the **People** sidebar section. This shows all registered vendor companies with their approval status.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Review Vendor Profiles">
    Click on a vendor to view their company details, certifications, past contract history, and current status.
  </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout variant="info" title="Vendor Registration">
  Vendors self-register through the public portal. Once registered, their application appears as **Pending** and requires approval before they can apply for contracts.
</Callout>
