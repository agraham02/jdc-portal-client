## Contract Lifecycle

Every contract moves through a lifecycle of stages:

| Stage | Description |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Contract is being prepared, not yet visible to vendors |
| **Open** | Published and accepting vendor applications |
| **Closed** | No longer accepting applications, under review |
| **Awarded** | A vendor has been selected for the contract |

## Creating a New Contract

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Start the Creation Form">
    Navigate to **Contracts** and click the **New Contract** button. This opens the contract creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Basic Information">
    Enter the contract **title**, a detailed **description** of the work, and the **contract type** (e.g., Service, Supply, Construction).
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Requirements & Terms">
    Specify the **requirements** vendors must meet, the **budget range** or estimated value, and the **deadline** for applications.
  </Step>
  <Step number={4} title="Attach Documents">
    Upload any supporting documents such as specifications, scope of work documents, or terms and conditions. Files are scanned for security before being stored.
  </Step>
  <Step number={5} title="Review & Save">
    Review all details carefully. Save the contract as a **Draft** initially — you can publish it (change to **Open**) when ready.
  </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Draft First">
  Always save new contracts as **Draft** first. This lets you review and make adjustments before vendors can see it. Publish to **Open** only when you're satisfied with all details.
</Callout>

## Managing Contract Status

To change a contract's status:

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Open the Contract">
    Navigate to the contract detail page by clicking on the contract in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Change Status">
    Use the status controls on the contract detail page to transition the contract to the next stage (e.g., Draft → Open → Closed → Awarded).
  </Step>
</StepList>

## Reviewing Applications

When vendors apply to an open contract, you can review their submissions:

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="View Applications">
    On the contract detail page, scroll to the **Applications** section to see all vendor submissions.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Review Each Application">
    Click on an application to view the vendor's submission details, attached documents, and company profile.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Evaluate & Decide">
    Review each application against the contract requirements. You can approve, reject, or shortlist applications.
  </Step>
  <Step number={4} title="Award the Contract">
    Once you've selected a vendor, award the contract to them. This changes the contract status to **Awarded** and notifies the winning vendor.
  </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout variant="warning" title="Irreversible Actions">
  Awarding a contract is a significant action. Make sure you've thoroughly reviewed all applications before proceeding, as this action notifies all applicants of the outcome.
</Callout>
