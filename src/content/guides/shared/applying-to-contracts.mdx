## How Contract Applications Work

Open contracts published by the organization are available for eligible vendors to apply. You'll submit a proposal along with any required documents, and the review team will evaluate your application.

<Callout variant="info" title="Vendor Account Required">
    You must have an active vendor account with an **Approved** status to apply
    for contracts. Contact an administrator if you need access.
</Callout>

## Finding Open Contracts

<StepList>
    <Step number={1} title="Navigate to Contracts">
        Click **Contracts** in the sidebar to browse available contracts. Only
        contracts with an **Open** status are accepting applications.
    </Step>
    <Step number={2} title="Review Contract Details">
        Click on any contract to view the full details — description, required
        documents, deadlines, and any specific submission requirements.
    </Step>
    <Step number={3} title="Check Required Documents">
        Before applying, review the **Required Documents** section carefully.
        Make sure you have all necessary files ready before starting your
        application.
    </Step>
</StepList>

## Submitting an Application

<StepList>
    <Step number={1} title="Click Apply">
        On the contract detail page, click the **Apply** button. This opens the
        application form.
    </Step>
    <Step number={2} title="Write Your Proposal">
        Fill in the proposal section with your offer — include relevant
        experience, approach, timeline, and pricing as applicable.
    </Step>
    <Step number={3} title="Upload Required Documents">
        Attach all required documents. Each document type is listed — upload the
        corresponding file for each. Supported formats and size limits are
        shown.
    </Step>
    <Step number={4} title="Review and Submit">
        Double-check your proposal and documents, then click **Submit**. You'll
        receive a confirmation once your application is submitted.
    </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout variant="warning" title="Submissions Cannot Be Edited">
    Once submitted, your application cannot be modified. Review everything
    carefully before clicking Submit.
</Callout>

## Tracking Your Applications

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Go to My Applications">
    Navigate to **Contracts → My Applications** to see all contracts you've applied to.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Check Status">
    Each application shows its current status:

    | Status | Meaning |
    |---|---|
    | **Submitted** | Your application is received and awaiting review |
    | **In Review** | The review team is actively evaluating your proposal |
    | **Accepted** | Your application has been shortlisted |
    | **Rejected** | Your application was not selected |
    | **Awarded** | You won the contract |

  </Step>
</StepList>

## Tips for a Strong Application

- **Read the full contract** — Understand all requirements before applying.
- **Prepare documents in advance** — Have all required files ready in supported formats.
- **Be specific in your proposal** — Address the contract requirements directly rather than submitting generic text.
- **Submit early** — Don't wait until the last minute. Contracts can close at any time.
