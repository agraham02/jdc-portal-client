## Notification Inbox

The JDC Portal keeps you informed about important events through an integrated notification system.

### Accessing Your Inbox

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Open the Inbox">
    Click the **bell icon** (🔔) in the top-right corner of the header. This opens your notification inbox as a dropdown panel.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="View Notifications">
    Your most recent notifications appear in the dropdown. Each notification shows the type of event, a brief description, and when it occurred.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Full Notifications Page">
    For a complete view of all notifications, click **Notifications** in the sidebar under the **Application** section. This opens the full notifications page with search and filtering capabilities.
  </Step>
</StepList>

## Understanding Notification Types

You may receive notifications for various events depending on your role:

| Notification Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Account Updates** | Changes to your account status, role assignments |
| **Contract Activity** | New contracts posted, application status changes |
| **Vendor Updates** | Vendor approval/rejection, profile changes |
| **Employee Events** | Onboarding updates, status changes |
| **HR Resources** | New documents uploaded, resource updates |

## Managing Preferences

You can control which notifications you receive and how they are delivered.

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Go to Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings** from the sidebar footer, or click the gear icon.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Open Notification Preferences">
    Select the **Notifications** tab within the settings page.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Configure Channels">
    Toggle notifications on or off for different event types. You may be able to choose between **in-app** and **email** delivery for each category.
  </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Stay Focused">
  If you're receiving too many notifications, use the preferences page to turn off non-essential alerts while keeping critical ones active.
</Callout>

<Callout variant="info" title="Email Notifications">
  Email notifications are sent to the email address associated with your account. Make sure your email is up to date in your **Profile** settings.
</Callout>
