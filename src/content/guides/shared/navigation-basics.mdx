## Welcome to the JDC Portal

The JDC Portal is your central hub for managing employees, vendors, contracts, HR resources, and more. This guide will help you get comfortable navigating the application.

## The Sidebar

The **sidebar** on the left is your primary navigation tool. It is organized into sections based on your role:

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Opening & Closing the Sidebar">
    Click the **menu icon** (☰) in the top-left corner of the header to toggle the sidebar open or closed. On larger screens, the sidebar stays open by default.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Navigating Sections">
    The sidebar is divided into collapsible sections like **Application**, **People**, **Procurement**, **HR**, and more. Click on any section label to expand or collapse it.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Finding Your Pages">
    Each section contains links to specific pages. Only pages you have permission to access will appear — so your sidebar is personalized to your role.
  </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Quick Navigation">
  Look for the **Dashboard** link at the top of the sidebar — it's your home base with a summary of key information tailored to your role.
</Callout>

## Dark Mode

The JDC Portal supports both light and dark themes.

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Toggle the Theme">
    Look for the **sun/moon icon** in the top-right corner of the header bar.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Choose Your Preference">
    Click the icon to switch between light mode, dark mode, or system preference. Your choice is saved automatically.
  </Step>
</StepList>

## Your Profile

Your profile contains your personal information, account details, and security settings.

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Access Your Profile">
    Click **Profile** in the sidebar footer (at the bottom of the sidebar), or navigate to the **Account** section.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Edit Your Information">
    From your profile page, you can update your personal details, change your password, and view your account information.
  </Step>
</StepList>

## Notifications

Stay informed about important updates through the notification system.

<StepList>
  <Step number={1} title="Check Notifications">
    Click the **bell icon** in the header to open your notification inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Manage Preferences">
    Go to **Settings** → **Notifications** to configure which notifications you receive and how you receive them.
  </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout variant="info" title="Need More Help?">
  Visit the **Help & Guides** section in the sidebar to find role-specific guides, or try an **Interactive Tour** for a hands-on walkthrough of key features.
</Callout>
